Rework starts when we do not know what we already know and ask the same questions over and over again.

The Problem

Two things send research back to the start.

Gaps in time. We cannot see what past studies covered, so we re-ask questions and re-research areas we already understand.

Misalignment. When guide questions do not trace back to the plan goals, the findings drift and the study cannot answer what it set out to.

What It Is

A research assistant for UX designers who carry research work but are not trained researchers.

It walks you through the standard study flow, one step at a time.

Know. What we know, and how we know it.

Plan. Goals and research questions.

Guide. A conversation guide linked to those goals.

Findings, Report, Register. Then back into the repository.

Know First

Search a topic and see questions asked before, each with its source file and line. Sample data: fictional studies.

Catch Repeats

Paste your draft questions. See which ones the VA already asked.

Each draft is compared with past questions, and the wording match is shown.

Align The Guide

Every guide question says which plan goal it serves, or that it is a warm-up.

Goal 3 has no question testing it. That gap shows up before the first session.

How It Works

1. Know

Search past research and pin what you need.

2. Plan

Set your goals and research questions.

3. Guide

Draft questions and link each to a goal.

4. Report

Add findings, then download Markdown.

The Report

The report builds itself as a draft Markdown file, from the plan and findings you already entered.

Download it or copy it. Blanks show as NEEDS INPUT, so nothing is made up.
